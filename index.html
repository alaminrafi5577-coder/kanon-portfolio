About me

Engineer, entrepreneur & team builder

I hold a Master's degree in Software Engineering (Software Technology) from University of Malaya, ranked 59th in the world in the 2021 QS ranking. My research focuses on a Traffic Control System.

Today I serve as Managing Director of A&R Entrepreneur Resources Sdn. Bhd. in Malaysia and Vice Chairman of Yeepix Limited, a company we founded in 2026 to bridge Bangladeshi students, professionals and skilled workers with world-class international opportunities, while working in Malaysia as a System Administrator at UnisonTech Consulting Sdn. Bhd. in Kuala Lumpur. Earlier I worked part-time as a software developer at JSOFT Solution.

I am dedicated, proactive and happy to take on bigger responsibilities. I work closely with teams to deliver plans, resolve issues and solve technology problems that have a direct impact on organizations. Outside work I enjoy football, cricket and badminton, and I take part in volunteer activities.
